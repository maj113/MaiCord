Opus codec tables and encoder tuning data

MaiCord's Opus decoder and encoder (src/media/audio/opus, src/media/audio/opus_decoder.cpp
and src/media/audio/opus_encoder.cpp) are MaiCord's own code, written from the Opus
specification (RFC 6716, updated by RFC 8251). They contain the specification's tables (the
range coder distributions, the SILK codebooks, filters and resampler coefficients, the CELT
band layout, bit allocation, pulse cache and window), which every Opus implementation
shares, and encoder tuning data taken from libopus 1.6.1 (the SILK rate-to-quality table,
the long-term prediction codebook costs and gains, the complexity presets, and the
thresholds of the rate split, stereo and transient decisions). No libopus source file is
part of MaiCord. The specification's code components and libopus are licensed as
reproduced below.
