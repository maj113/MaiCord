MaiCord original notification sounds

message-alert.mcp and focused-message-alert.mcp were synthesized by MaiCord's
generator (tools/generate_message_alerts.py, now in the repository history) using mathematical signals. No recordings,
Discord assets, or third-party audio are used as inputs.

The voice connection sounds (join, leave and connection lost) have no file. MaiCord synthesizes them at run time from
the note table in src/app/notifications/sound_assets.cpp: sine tones with one harmonic and a decay. No recordings,
Discord assets, or third-party audio are used as inputs.
