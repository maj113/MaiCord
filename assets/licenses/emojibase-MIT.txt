Emojibase shortcodes

MaiCord's emoji shortcode table (src/ui/emoji_shortcodes.inc) includes
alias names from emojibase-data 16.0.3, en/shortcodes/joypixels.json,
iamcal.json and emojibase.json:
https://github.com/milesj/emojibase
https://www.npmjs.com/package/emojibase-data

The joypixels preset comes from JoyPixels' emoji-toolkit, whose non-artwork
files (including its emoji data) are MIT licensed; no JoyPixels artwork is
used: https://github.com/joypixels/emoji-toolkit
The iamcal preset comes from emoji-data, MIT licensed, Copyright (c) 2013
Cal Henderson: https://github.com/iamcal/emoji-data
