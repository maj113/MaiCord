PyThaiNLP Thai word-list data

MaiCord's Thai word-break lexicon (src/text/generated/thai_lexical_data.inc)
includes the words of the PyThaiNLP project's words_th.txt that are not
compounds of other listed words, re-encoded by tools/unicode/thai_break_data.py.
No PyThaiNLP library or implementation code is included in the application.

The upstream corpus license identifies words_th.txt as created by the PyThaiNLP
project and released under Creative Commons Zero 1.0 Universal Public Domain
Dedication (CC0-1.0).
